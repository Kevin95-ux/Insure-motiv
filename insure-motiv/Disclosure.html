06

Your choice as a consumer

You are not required to purchase a policy or any other product merely because your inquiry is routed or referred to a participating company.

Before authorizing a project, consider independently confirming:

  • The provider’s full legal identity and contact information.
  • Applicable contractor registration, licensing, bonding, insurance, and permit responsibilities.
  • Written measurements, specifications, product details, installation scope, pricing, taxes, fees, and exclusions.
  • Deposit requirements, cancellation rights, financing terms, payment schedules, and refund policies.
  • Product, glass, frame, hardware, workmanship, labor, and installation warranties.
  • Estimated manufacturing, delivery, installation, and completion timelines.
  • Who will perform the work and whether subcontractors may be used.
  • Whether another estimate or provider comparison would be appropriate.

You may decline an estimate, request additional information, compare alternatives, choose another provider, or decide not to proceed.
