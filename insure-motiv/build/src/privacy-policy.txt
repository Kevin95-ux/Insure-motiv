This Privacy Policy explains how Motiv Brands, Inc., Leads Motiv, LLC, and their controlled subsidiaries and affiliated consumer-facing brands (collectively, "Motiv," "we," "us," or "our") collect, use, disclose, and otherwise process personal information.

Insure Motiv is a consumer-facing marketing brand and DBA of Leads Motiv, LLC, a subsidiary of Motiv Brands, Inc.

Motiv operates a network of consumer-facing websites, landing pages, forms, marketing brands, and related services. Leads Motiv, LLC is the primary entity through which many of Motiv's consumer-facing marketing brands and DBAs are used for lead generation. A current list of our subsidiaries, brands, and DBAs is available through our Brands, Affiliates, and Marketing Partners Disclosure.

This Privacy Policy applies to information collected through our websites, landing pages, forms, telephone communications, text messages, emails, advertising interactions, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

Not every Motiv brand collects every category of information described in this Policy. The information collected depends on the Service you request and the information you choose to provide.

H2 1. OUR BUSINESS MODEL

Motiv operates consumer-facing brands that help individuals request information, services, quotes, qualification reviews, consultations, or connections with service providers.

Depending on the Service, we may: provide a service directly; help determine whether a consumer may qualify for or be interested in a particular service; match a consumer with an appropriate service provider; transmit a consumer's request to a selected service provider; facilitate communications between consumers and providers; or receive compensation in connection with a consumer-requested referral or lead.

When we provide a lead to a third-party service provider, the completed lead is intended to be provided exclusively to one selected provider for the specific service requested by the consumer, unless we clearly disclose otherwise and obtain any required authorization.

We may use automated routing, matching, ping/post, availability, or bidding technology to identify an appropriate provider. Limited information may therefore be processed through routing systems or prospective providers to determine eligibility, availability, geographic coverage, pricing, or fit before the completed lead is delivered to the selected provider.

Our agreements generally prohibit a selected provider from reselling or redistributing a Motiv lead to another lead buyer.

A current list of companies that may receive consumer leads is available through our Marketing Partners Disclosure.

H3 B. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit or interact with our Services, we and our technology providers may automatically collect: IP address; browser type; device type; operating system; device or advertising identifiers; pages viewed; links clicked; referral URLs; date and time of interactions; advertising attribution information; approximate geographic location derived from your IP address, state, or ZIP code; cookie and tracking technology identifiers; session and website interaction information; and information regarding how you arrived at and interacted with our Services.

We do not collect GPS-based precise device location through our lead forms unless we separately disclose that practice.

H3 C. Consent and Compliance Records

When you submit a form, provide communications consent, or otherwise interact with our Services, we may preserve records demonstrating what you requested and what disclosures were presented to you.

These records may include: date and time of submission; IP address; page or URL where the submission occurred; referring page or advertisement; browser and user-agent information; form responses; version of the form; exact disclosure language presented; checkbox selections; consent status; consent timestamp; applicable provider or partner information; TrustedForm or similar consent-certification records; revocation or opt-out records; and Do-Not-Call suppression information.

We maintain these records for compliance, auditing, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and evidence of consumer instructions and consent.

H2 3. INFORMATION WE INTENTIONALLY DO NOT COLLECT

Our lead-generation Services are designed to avoid collecting unnecessary highly sensitive information.

Through our ordinary consumer lead forms, we do not intentionally request: Social Security numbers; bank account numbers; credit or debit card numbers; financial-account login credentials; detailed banking information; detailed financial records; credit reports or credit scores; government identification numbers; or health information.

Where financing interest is relevant, we generally request a general budget or credit-range category rather than detailed financial records.

Insure Motiv does not request medical, disability, or health information through its lead forms. A licensed agent or carrier may ask health questions separately, after you are connected; any such information is collected by that agent or carrier under their own privacy practices, not through Insure Motiv.

H2 4. COVERAGE AND ELIGIBILITY INFORMATION

Certain Insure Motiv Services request information regarding your property, your homeownership status, and the coverage you are considering.

We collect coverage and eligibility information when it is reasonably necessary to: evaluate preliminary qualification criteria; respond to your request; identify an appropriate licensed insurance agent or carrier; confirm that a provider services your area; route your request to the selected provider; facilitate the service you requested; maintain evidence of your request and authorization; and comply with applicable law.

State and ZIP code are used to determine where a product is available and which agents are licensed in your state. We do not use your address to derive precise device geolocation.

We may use the fact that a person visited or interacted with the Insure Motiv website for ordinary site-level advertising or retargeting, subject to applicable privacy choices.

Depending on where you live, some categories of information we collect may be considered personal information or sensitive data under applicable law. We process such information only as described in this Policy and as permitted by applicable law.

H2 7. COMPENSATED REFERRALS, "SALE," AND "SHARING"

Motiv is a lead-generation business and may receive compensation when we successfully connect a consumer with a service provider.

We may therefore disclose or transfer personal information to a selected provider in connection with the service you requested, and Motiv may receive compensation in connection with that referral.

Certain state privacy laws define terms such as "sell," "sale," or "sharing" more broadly than those terms are commonly used. As a result, certain compensated disclosures or advertising activities described in this Policy may be treated as a sale or sharing of personal information under applicable law.

We do not interpret the word "sale" to mean that consumers themselves are purchasing anything from Motiv. Our consumer lead-generation Services are generally provided without charge to the consumer.

Depending on applicable law: a consumer-requested disclosure to a provider may be exempt from certain sale definitions; a compensated referral may nevertheless be treated as a sale; disclosure of online identifiers for cross-context behavioral or targeted advertising may constitute sharing or targeted advertising; and consumers may have rights to opt out of these activities.

Where required, we provide a Your Privacy Choices mechanism through which eligible consumers may exercise applicable opt-out rights.

We also honor recognized browser-based universal opt-out preference signals, including Global Privacy Control, where required by applicable law.

An opt-out does not necessarily apply to a disclosure that you intentionally direct us to make in order to fulfill a service request where applicable law excludes that disclosure from the definition of sale or sharing.

H2 8. COOKIES, PIXELS, AND TRACKING TECHNOLOGIES

These technologies may be used to: remember preferences; operate websites; measure visits; understand traffic sources; attribute conversions; measure advertising campaigns; prevent fraud; improve performance; personalize marketing; and conduct targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising where permitted.

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to control certain technologies through our cookie or privacy controls, your browser settings, Global Privacy Control, or our Your Privacy Choices mechanism.

H2 12. DATA RETENTION

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to legal, operational, contractual, and compliance requirements.

Our general retention framework is:

- Ordinary lead records: generally up to 24 months after the last relevant activity;

- Telemarketing and communications-consent evidence: generally at least 5 years where maintained for legal or compliance purposes;

- Call and communications records: for the period reasonably necessary for compliance, quality assurance, dispute resolution, and applicable recordkeeping requirements;

- Opt-out and Do-Not-Call records: we may retain a limited suppression record for longer periods so that we can continue honoring your request; and

- Other information: for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected or as required by law.

When information is no longer reasonably necessary, we may delete, de-identify, aggregate, or otherwise dispose of it.

A deletion request may therefore result in deletion of the underlying lead while we retain limited information necessary to document and continue honoring a Do-Not-Call, opt-out, fraud-prevention, or legal-compliance request.
